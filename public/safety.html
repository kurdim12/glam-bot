Safety Guidelines

Last updated 1 January 2026 · GLAMBOT Jordan, Amman

Why these rules exist

Our unit is a 147 kg powered robotic arm that moves at speed close to your guests. It is an impressive shot, and it is also a piece of moving machinery. These guidelines exist so that everyone around it — guests, staff and our own operator — stays safe.

The four hard requirements

Every venue we work at needs to meet these before we can operate:

  • A minimum clear height of 3 m above the shooting area
  • A floor rated to support 147 kg at the arm's base position
  • At least 5 m² of clear space for the unit to move in
  • 20 minutes of exclusive access to the space for setup and again for teardown

The exclusion zone

While the arm is running, there is an exclusion zone immediately around it. Only our operator may enter that zone. Guests, staff and photographers should stay outside it, even if the arm appears to be paused.

Operator authority

Our operator has final say over how and when the arm runs. They can pause or stop it at any time, for any reason, including if the crowd moves closer than is safe or if conditions change.

Floor and access

The unit needs level, solid flooring. Steps, sand, gravel and soft grass under the base are not suitable. We also need a route wide enough to wheel the unit in and out of the venue without lifting it over obstacles.

Power

We need a dedicated grounded outlet near the operating position. Any cables running across walkways must be taped down or ramped so nobody trips over them.

Outdoor and weather rules

We do not operate in rain or on standing water, and we have wind limits beyond which the arm cannot run safely. If your shoot is outdoors in direct summer heat, please provide shade for the unit and our operator.

Guests, children and drinks

Please keep children away from the unit and the exclusion zone at all times. We also ask that drinks and food are kept clear of the arm and its base to avoid spills near the equipment.

What we ask of your event staff

On the day, we ask your planner or venue staff to help us keep the exclusion zone clear during operation, point us to the confirmed power source, and give us the agreed setup and teardown windows without other activity competing for the same space.
