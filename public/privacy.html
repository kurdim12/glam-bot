Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 January 2026 · GLAMBOT Jordan, Amman

What this covers

This policy explains what information GLAMBOT Jordan (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website or book a shoot with us, why we collect it, and what choices you have. It applies to this site and to the enquiries that reach us through it.

Information you give us

When you submit the booking form we receive only what you type into it:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp or phone number, including the country code you select
  • Your email address
  • The occasion, shoot date, and location
  • Any additional comments you choose to add

We use these details to reply to you, confirm the date and venue, and plan the shoot. We do not need or ask for payment card details through this website.

Information collected automatically

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser loads a web font from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request — the same as on most websites. If you would rather avoid that, blocking third-party font loading in your browser will not break the site.

Footage and photographs

The clips we film at your event are the reason you hired us, so they get their own rules. We deliver your footage to you. We keep a working copy for a limited period so we can re-deliver files if something is lost. We only publish a clip publicly — on our website, our Instagram, or in a pitch — when the person or organisation shown has agreed to it. If you have agreed and later change your mind, write to us and we will take it down.

Who else sees your information

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for marketing. We share it only where it is necessary to do the job: with the operator assigned to your event, with an editor working on your footage, and with our email or messaging providers as part of ordinary delivery. Anyone working with us is bound to the same confidentiality we are.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept while we are in conversation and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can pick the thread back up. Records tied to a completed booking are kept as long as accounting and tax rules in Jordan require. After that they are deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time and we will stop. Send any of these requests to book@glambotjo.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take sensible precautions to protect what you send us, including limiting who can access enquiry details and footage. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please do not send us sensitive personal documents through this form.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be flagged on the site.
